# ColorPickr

The color tool for Mac designers. Pick any color on screen, build palettes, mood boards and design systems, check contrast and export to code.

## What's new in 0.21.0

- **A clear way back** from the color picker: **‹ Back** on the color strip at the top, or click the strip. When you've changed the color, **Use This Color** sits right there too.
- **Contrast on your own background.** Under Readable as text?, a third check tests your color on a background you choose: pick it from the screen, choose it from a palette, or right-click a History swatch. Same verdict, ratio and Fix as the white and black checks, and the panel remembers it.
