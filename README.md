## What's new in 0.14.0

- **Design System:** one brand color becomes brand, neutral, success, warning, error and info scales that belong together. Status colors are checked so they never look alike to color-blind people. Export to Tailwind v4, shadcn/ui, Figma variables or CSS.
- **Screen Check** (⌃⌥S): drag over any design, or click a window, and see every line of text marked easy or hard to read, with a fix that keeps the hue. It also flags colors that look alike to color-blind people and lists the colors the design really uses. Works on images too.
- **ColorPickr Pro.** The picker, formats, history, contrast checks, color vision, palettes, the Color Wheel, and CSS and CSV export stay free. Pro adds mood boards, themes, colors from a website, Palette Studio, the floating palette, brand sheets and every other export.
